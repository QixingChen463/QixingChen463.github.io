Wordle, with the solver showing its work

The game below is the Java project from my wordle repository, ported to the browser. Play it as usual, or leave the solver panel open: before each guess it scores all 2315 words by how much they are expected to tell you, and after each guess it shows what the colours actually cost the field. Played out over every word in the list, choosing by that score alone solves all 2315 in six guesses or fewer, averaging 3.50.

A guess is worth a certain number of bits on average; the colouring that comes back is worth log2(before / after). Getting more than expected means the answer fell in a small bucket, which is luck, not a better guess.

How it picks

A guess splits the words still possible into buckets, one per colouring it could come back as. If a bucket holds a fraction p of them, seeing that colouring carries log2(1/p) bits, so the guess is worth

H = −∑ p log2 p

bits on average, and the higher H is, the fewer words are expected to survive. Every word is scored as a guess, not just the ones that could still be the answer: a word that cannot possibly be right may still split the field better. Ties go to a word that could be the answer, since that one might just win.

On an empty board the ranking is the same every game, so this page ships it rather than making you wait: RAISE at 5.88 bits, then SLATE, CRATE, IRATE, TRACE. After that the candidate set is small enough that scoring all 2315 words against it takes a few milliseconds.

What gray means

The part that is easy to get wrong. Gray does not mean the letter is absent, it means there are no copies of it beyond the ones already coloured in the same guess. Guessing SPEED against ABIDE colours the first E yellow and the second gray, because ABIDE has one E and it is already accounted for. Reading gray as "no E anywhere" would throw away the right answer whenever it repeats a letter, so the colouring runs in two passes, greens claimed first.

Against the alternatives

Every word in the list played out, guesses capped at six, from the benchmark in the repository:

Strategy Opens with Average Worst Over six
letter frequency alert 3.684 8 22 of 2315
entropy (this page) raise 3.496 6 none
decision-tree search, beam 25 slate 3.444 6 none

Entropy is optimal for one turn but not for the game: a guess that reveals slightly fewer bits now can leave a set that splits better next turn, which is why the search opens with SLATE instead and finishes a twentieth of a guess sooner. The search is in the Java project; it is too slow to run while you wait, so the page uses the entropy strategy.

Both numbers are above the published optimum of 3.4212 (Selby, 2016), which comes from searching the full 12972-word guess list. This project only guesses from the 2315 answers, so it cannot reach that.
